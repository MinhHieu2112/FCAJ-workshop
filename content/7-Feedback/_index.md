> Below are my genuine reflections, overall evaluation, and feedback after completing the 2-month **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited** (from June 22, 2026 to August 15, 2026).

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at AWS Vietnam is exceptionally professional, open, and inspiring. Innovation is deeply encouraged, and new interns are warmly welcomed. The modern workspace and enterprise-grade tooling allow interns to integrate smoothly and maximize productivity.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admin**  
The mentors and admin team of the First Cloud AI Journey program provided outstanding support. They offered structured learning roadmaps, promptly resolved complex AWS queries, and empowered interns to independently research, test solutions, and learn from hands-on challenges.

**3. Relevance of Tasks to Academic Major**  
The internship scope perfectly bridged Application Engineering (NestJS, Next.js) with Cloud Computing (AWS Cloud). Directly integrating enterprise services like Amazon Cognito, Amazon S3 Presigned URLs, and Amazon Location Service into the Real Estate Rental Management System allowed me to consolidate academic fundamentals with industry-grade execution.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The Bootcamp delivered immense educational value: from mastering Agile/Scrum workflows and project tracking via Worklogs & Proposals, to adopting the *AWS Well-Architected Framework*. Additionally, my technical writing and workshop documentation skills improved dramatically.

**5. Corporate Culture & Team Spirit**  
AWS Leadership Principles (such as *Customer Obsession*, *Learn and Be Curious*, and *Ownership*) are genuinely embodied every day. Fellow FCAJ interns actively shared knowledge, assisted one another during challenging labs, and successfully hit project milestones on schedule.

**6. Internship Benefits & Support**  
The program provided comprehensive AWS cloud resources (AWS Credits/Accounts), official learning materials, and flexible scheduling to help interns maintain a healthy balance between university commitments and internship responsibilities.

---

### Key Feedback Questions

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  Being granted full ownership to design, build, and document an end-to-end AWS Workshop guide for a real-world SaaS project under expert mentor guidance.

- **What do you think the program could improve for future cohorts?**  
  Introducing interactive *Mock Architecture Review* sessions with AWS Cloud Solution Architects so interns can practice defending technical decisions in front of experts.

- **Would you recommend this internship program to your peers? Why?**  
  **Absolutely yes.** This is one of the premier internship experiences for students passionate about Cloud Computing and AI, offering an unmatched career springboard and global industry perspective.

---

### Suggestions & Future Expectations

- **Suggestions for Improvement**: Organize informal tech talks or coffee chats connecting Bootcamp interns with AWS engineers across diverse domains (Solutions Architects, DevOps, AI/ML specialists).
- **Future Expectations**: Eager to explore future graduate or full-time opportunities with AWS, and continue contributing to the local AWS User Group community.
