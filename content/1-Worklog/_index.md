## Introduction

This worklog documents the entire internship process over **8 weeks**, from **June 22, 2026** to **August 15, 2026**. The activities are organized by week (Monday to Friday), providing an overview of the tasks completed, the results achieved, and the practical knowledge gained throughout the internship.

The internship followed a structured roadmap, beginning with onboarding and familiarization with AWS core services, followed by business requirements analysis, architectural design of the Real Estate Rental Management System, modular feature development, migrating the database to Amazon RDS, implementing real-time chat, optimizing database performance, and strengthening application security.

The detailed work completed each week is presented below:

- **Week 1:** [Onboarding and Introduction to AWS Core Services](1.1-week1/)
- **Week 2:** [Requirements Analysis, Architecture Design, and Amazon Cognito Integration](1.2-week2/)
- **Week 3:** [Developing Tenant, Manager, Lease, and Application Business Modules](1.3-week3/)
- **Week 4:** [Developing Property Module and Search/Filter Functionality](1.4-week4/)
- **Week 5:** [Completing Manager Dashboard, Property Editing, and Migrating DB to Amazon RDS](1.5-week5/)
- **Week 6:** [Developing Real-Time Chat and Amazon SES Notification Integration](1.6-week6/)
- **Week 7:** [System Performance Optimization, Handling N+1 Queries, Race Conditions, and AWS Location Service Integration](1.7-week7/)
- **Week 8:** [Comprehensive Testing, Technical Documentation, Proposal Completion, and System Handover](1.8-week8/)
